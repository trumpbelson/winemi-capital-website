Stewardship

Asset Management

Portfolio management is understanding how investments interact and how the overall portfolio behaves under different market conditions.

These principles form a framework for thinking about portfolios. They are educational and do not imply that WINEMI CAPITAL. currently offers discretionary portfolio management to the public.

Investment Objectives

Clear goals — growth, income, preservation, or a blend — drive every subsequent decision.

Risk Tolerance & Time Horizons

The capacity and willingness to accept variability in outcomes. When capital will be needed determines how much short-term volatility can be tolerated.

Liquidity Needs

Portfolios must fund known and contingent cash requirements without forced sales at unfavourable prices.

Asset Allocation & Diversification

The division of capital among asset classes is typically the dominant driver of long-term portfolio behaviour.

Portfolio Construction & Research

Selecting and sizing individual holdings within the allocation framework, mindful of correlation, concentration, and costs.

Performance, Monitoring & Rebalancing

Returns must be evaluated relative to objectives, risk taken, and appropriate benchmarks. Portfolios drift; regular review maintains the intended risk profile.

Capital Preservation & Risk-adjusted Returns

For some objectives, avoiding permanent loss of capital takes priority over maximising return.
