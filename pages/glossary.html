Glossary

Shared language for structure, imbalance, and risk — so Education, Insights, and live labs mean the same thing.

Base

A pause or consolidation after a directional move. Quality rises when the range is relatively tight and orderly before an impulsive departure.

Demand zone

An area where aggressive buying may have absorbed sell flow — often marked by a base before a strong rally (e.g. DBR or RBR context).

Supply zone

An area where aggressive selling may have absorbed buy flow — often marked by a base before a strong drop (e.g. RBD or DBD context).

DBR

Drop–Base–Rally. A demand reversal pattern: decline, base, impulsive rally. First return to the base is a candidate demand area, not a guarantee.

RBR

Rally–Base–Rally. Demand continuation: advance, base, continued advance. Pullbacks into the base may be demand candidates if structure holds.

RBD

Rally–Base–Drop. Supply reversal: advance, base, impulsive drop. Revisits from below may offer supply context.

DBD

Drop–Base–Drop. Supply continuation: decline, base, continued decline. Rallies into the base may meet continuation supply.

Freshness

How often a zone has been tested. Untested or lightly tested areas are generally higher quality than zones already revisited many times.

Invalidation

The level or condition that proves the hypothesis wrong. Written before targets; used to size risk.

Higher-timeframe (HTF)

The larger chart context (e.g. daily/weekly) that filters lower-timeframe patterns.

Imbalance

A stretch of price where one side dominated — often visible as impulsive range expansion or inefficient two-way trade.

Absorption

Opposing orders taking the other side of flow without immediate continuation — can precede reversal or mark strong interest.

Liquidity

The ability to transact size without excessive slippage. Session depth and event risk change effective liquidity around levels.

Regime

The prevailing market condition — e.g. trending rally, trending drop, or balanced/range — that frames pattern quality.

Discount / Premium

Relative location within a higher-timeframe range. Demand ideas often preferred in discount; supply ideas in premium — as a bias, not a rule.

Risk budget

How much loss a book or idea is allowed to take. Size is derived from invalidation distance and the risk budget, not from conviction alone.
