Glossary

Shared language for structure, zones, and risk — used across Education and Insights.

A price area where aggressive buying previously overwhelmed selling, often identified by a base and impulsive rally away (DBR / RBR).
A price area where aggressive selling previously overwhelmed buying, often identified by a base and impulsive drop away (RBD / DBD).
A period of relatively tight consolidation before an impulsive departure. The origin of many supply/demand maps.
A zone not yet revisited since formation. Often studied with higher priority than multi-tested levels — still not a guarantee.
The price level or condition where a zone hypothesis is treated as wrong. Defined before targets in a disciplined process.
The sequence of aggressive and passive orders. Education uses observable proxies rather than claims of hidden intent.
The larger structural map that filters lower-timeframe ideas — discount vs premium location.
Resting orders and areas where stops or interest may cluster. Educational concept — not a precise retail readout of institutional books.
