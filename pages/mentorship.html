Development

Mentorship

Structured guidance in markets, investing, risk, and financial discipline. Mentorship does not guarantee trading or investment success.

WINEMI CAPITAL. intends to support learners through progressive pathways — from foundations to practical application. Programmes may include guided study, discussion of market concepts, risk frameworks, and disciplined process design.

Enrolment details, schedules, fees, and mentor assignments are not published here until operational. Use the Contact form to express interest.

Beginner

For those new to markets or formal investing. Covers financial markets basics, investing fundamentals, risk concepts, and financial discipline. No prior market experience required.

Intermediate

For those with foundational knowledge seeking depth. Covers portfolio concepts, market research, trading education, and risk management. Recommended: completed foundations or equivalent experience.

Advanced

For experienced participants refining process. Covers quantitative analysis, trading psychology, advanced risk frameworks, and research methodology.

Important: Mentorship does not guarantee trading or investment success. Financial markets involve risk of loss.
