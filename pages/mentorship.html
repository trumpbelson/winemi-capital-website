Mentorship

Guided development of chart literacy, process discipline, and decision hygiene — not a signal service.

What mentorship is

A structured path to recognise market structure, define risk, and build review habits. Terms confirmed directly. Public site content remains educational.

Pathway pillars

Literacy

Trends, bases, four patterns (DBR, RBR, RBD, DBD), zone quality, higher-timeframe context.

Process

Checklists, written invalidation, journaling, and post-session review.

Risk behaviour

Sizing, survival through losing sequences, discipline to stand aside when incomplete.

Who it suits

  • Learners ready to practise on charts, not only consume content
  • Professionals who want institutional vocabulary without hype
  • Anyone willing to define risk before debating entries

Who it does not suit

  • Anyone seeking guaranteed income or copy-trade signals
  • Those unwilling to journal or accept invalidation
  • Requests for personalised investment advice via public channels
