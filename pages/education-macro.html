Macro & data desk

Policy paths, growth dispersion, and the data calendar — read next to structure, not instead of it. This desk is educational context only; it is not a forecast or a solicitation.

How to use this desk

Scan the calendar for high-impact releases. Note whether quiet bases sit in front of those events. Cross-check equities against yields, the dollar, and gold. Invalidation still belongs on the price structure — macro only sets the backdrop.

Desk workflow

  1. Calendar first. Identify high-impact releases in the next 1–5 sessions (CPI, labour, PMIs, central banks).
  2. Map the backdrop. Check US 10Y, DXY, and major equity indices for regime — risk-on, risk-off, or mixed.
  3. Structure second. Only then open HTF zones and candles. Event risk is part of zone quality.
  4. Write invalidation. If a base sits into a major print, define what would break the structure before the print, not after.
