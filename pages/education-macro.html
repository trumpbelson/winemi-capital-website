How to use this desk

Scan the calendar for high-impact releases. Check the markets board and focus chart for regime. Cross-check equities against yields, the dollar, and gold. Invalidation still belongs on price structure.

Desk workflow

  1. Calendar first. Identify high-impact releases in the next 1–5 sessions.
  2. Map the backdrop. Check US 10Y, DXY, and major equity indices for regime.
  3. Structure second. Only then open HTF zones and candles.
  4. Write invalidation. Define what would break the structure before a major print.

Study themes

Policy reaction function

How markets price the next moves in rates often matters as much as the level of rates itself.

Growth dispersion

Regions diverge. Relative growth shows up in FX, equity leadership, and commodity demand.

Event risk

Treat major data and central-bank days as part of zone quality.

Dollar & yields

Rising DXY with rising real yields often pressures gold and emerging FX; confirm with structure.

Liquidity lens

Balance-sheet paths change move amplitude. Macro does not replace candle invalidation.
