Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell, or an offer of a fund or managed account. It is literacy about asset classes and process.

Equities

Ownership claims on productive assets. Sensitive to growth, rates, and earnings. Structure and breadth matter.

Fixed income

Rates, credit, and duration as building blocks — with their own volatility when policy shifts.

Commodities & real assets

Energy, metals, and related exposures that can diversify or amplify inflation and geopolitical shocks.

FX

Relative growth and policy differentials — often the transmission channel for global liquidity.

Digital assets

High-volatility instruments requiring explicit risk limits; education does not remove market risk.

Process questions

Horizon, liquidity needs, drawdown tolerance, concentration, and rebalance triggers — before product labels.
