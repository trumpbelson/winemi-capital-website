Markets

Genuine candlestick charts with OHLC data for major indices, commodities, foreign exchange, and digital assets. Data provided by TradingView. Availability, delay, and market hours vary by instrument and exchange.

Source: TradingView · Candlestick · Multiple timeframes available in chart toolbar

About the data

Charts on this page use the TradingView advanced chart widget. Symbols are clearly identified. Data may be real-time or delayed according to the rules of the underlying exchange or data provider. Cryptocurrency pairs (e.g. BTC/USDT) typically update more frequently; index and equity data may be delayed for non-professional users.

If a chart fails to load, the cause is usually network restriction, ad-blocking, or temporary unavailability of the widget. No random or fabricated prices are generated on this site.

Instruments displayed: NASDAQ-100 via QQQ (NASDAQ:QQQ), Gold (TVC:GOLD), Bitcoin (BINANCE:BTCUSDT), S&P 500 (TVC:SPX), Dow Jones (TVC:DJI), EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, WTI Crude Oil (TVC:USOIL).
