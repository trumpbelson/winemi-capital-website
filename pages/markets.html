Asset desks (study framing)

Equities

Index structure, leadership vs breadth, and whether advances are accepted on higher timeframes. Watch how equities respond when yields and the dollar move together.

Rates & dollar

Policy paths and real-yield pressure often set the tone for risk assets and gold. DXY and US 10Y are practical anchors on this desk.

FX majors

Relative growth and rate differentials expressed through pairs. Clean bases still need event awareness around data and central banks.

Commodities

Gold as a hedge and dollar foil; oil as a growth and geopolitical pulse. Impulsive departures from bases can reprice inflation expectations quickly.

Volatility

VIX is a regime label, not a timing oracle. High-vol environments widen noise around levels — demand stronger departure and clearer HTF alignment.

Digital assets

High-beta instruments with operational and market risk. Treat structure study with explicit size discipline; education does not remove technology risk.

Session awareness

Liquidity and narrative leadership rotate across the day. Structure still comes first — session context only changes how much noise to expect around a level.

Asia

Often sets early tone in FX and regional equities; watch for continuation or fade into Europe.

Europe

Deep FX and rates liquidity; data and policy speakers can expand ranges before the US open.

North America

US equities, dollar, and policy calendar frequently dominate global risk sentiment into the close.

Recommended study workflow

Note which assets are leading or lagging. A strong equity tape with a firm dollar and rising yields is a different environment than risk-on with a soft dollar.
Use the instrument chips. Mark higher-timeframe structure first, then look for bases and impulsive departures. Avoid treating every wick as institutional intent.
Open Macro & data for high-impact releases. Quiet bases into major events can fail violently — event risk is part of zone quality.
Before debating targets, mark where the idea is wrong. Visit the Risk lab for volatility context and sizing education.
Write the structure story in one paragraph: regime, location, what would invalidate. Process improves faster than memory.
