Zone quality checklist

A zone never revisited since formation often carries more weight than one chopped through many times. Freshness is a quality score — not a promise of reversal.
Impulsive, wide-range candles leaving a tight base suggest imbalance. Prefer departure that also breaks nearby structure where possible.
Lower-timeframe demand inside higher-timeframe discount is a different hypothesis than the same zone against higher-timeframe supply.
Demand ideas are often preferred in discount relative to the HTF range; supply ideas in premium. Location is a bias filter, not a standalone rule.

Order flow — practical observables

  • Time & sales — sequence of prints
  • Depth / book — resting liquidity
  • Footprint / volume profile — where volume clustered
  • Delta / absorption ideas — hypothesis only
Order-flow reads without HTF structure become noise. Use flow to refine timing only after the zone and invalidation are defined.
