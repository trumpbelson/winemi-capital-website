Orientation

Research means organised observation: what the chart has shown, what the calendar is pricing, and what would force a view to be abandoned.

Three working lenses

Structure lens

Trend regime, bases, zones, fresh vs depleted levels, higher-timeframe alignment.

Macro lens

Policy paths, growth dispersion, energy and geopolitical premiums, high-impact releases.

Risk lens

Volatility regime, invalidation, crowding, and size that survives failed hypotheses.

Recurring study themes

Quiet bases ahead of major data can fail violently. Note the calendar before treating a zone as high quality on event day.
