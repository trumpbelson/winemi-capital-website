How we frame market questions: structure first, macro context second, narrative last.

Orientation

Public research-style notes are educational. They describe frameworks and observed conditions — they are not forecasts, target prices, or solicitations.

Structure lens

Trend regime, bases, zones, and whether price is accepting or rejecting key areas on the relevant timeframe.

Macro lens

Policy paths, growth dispersion, energy and geopolitical premiums, and calendar risk around major releases.

Risk lens

Volatility regime, invalidation levels, crowding, and what would force a view to be abandoned.
