Method

Our approach

Process before narrative. Structure before prediction. Risk defined before size. Built for literacy and disciplined decision hygiene — not for selling certainty.

Core stance

Markets are systems of imbalance, liquidity, and information under time pressure. We study what price has already done, where residual orders may still sit, and what would invalidate a hypothesis — before debating stories about why.

1 · Structure first

Identify regime, locate bases, map demand and supply from impulsive departures. Higher-timeframe context filters noise.

2 · Location quality

Freshness, strength of departure, and discount/premium alignment change zone quality.

3 · Confirmation, not hope

A complete setup is a checklist — pattern, location, confirmation, defined risk — not a feeling.

4 · Macro as context

Policy paths, growth dispersion, energy shocks, and the calendar can expand ranges and invalidate quiet bases.

5 · Risk is design

Invalidation is written before targets. Size so a string of failed ideas is survivable.

6 · Process over outcome

Optimise for repeatable decision quality, not for yesterday’s P&L narrative.

What we deliberately avoid

  • Guaranteed outcomes or “set and forget” claims
  • Treating every swing as institutional intent
  • Confusing public education with personalised advice
  • Crowding into themes without breadth or invalidation
